# Audio

Recitation of al-Arba'un al-Nawawiyya with Ibn Rajab's additions (50 hadith), read by **Ibrahim al-Ansari** (إبراهيم الأنصاري).

- Source: IslamHouse — https://islamhouse.com/ar/audios/4458/
  (file `ar_AlArb3un_AlNuweh_AlAnsaree.mp3`, © www.islamhouse.com)
- IslamHouse allows republishing its materials with attribution and without changing the content.
- Processing: the single 42:44 recording was split at the start of each hadith heading
  (`segments.json`, seconds in the original file) and re-encoded to AAC-LC 48 kbps mono
  (`h01.m4a` … `h50.m4a`). Nothing was cut out, reordered or added; hadith 1 also contains the
  short opening (basmala) of the recording.
